FIELD NOTE
Scarcity is writing the checks. Sometimes over the character.

Torchic outsold Charizard nearly 7 to 1 in PSA 10 at Alt last month. This month a PSA 8 Trophy Pikachu passed $1M in pre-bids because it’s the only one PSA has graded. Character still sells cards, but at the top of this market, scarcity can overpower character popularity, and even grade.
